## Description

ACAPS rates humanitarian risks, but the people closest to a crisis have no simple way to say what an analysis misses, and ACAPS cannot learn whose judgement holds up.

Ground Truth gives a small, approved network of experts a card for each risk in their countries or domains. In a few minutes they agree or disagree, comment and forecast how likely the risk is. Votes from country experts count for more, and when a chat closes everyone sees the share who agree next to a separate country line. ACAPS moderators get a desktop view: a map of open risks, a page per country, each risk's answers grouped into claims with the best-matched experts first, links between risks with a stated cause, expert approvals, and new risks posted to the experts who match them. Each expert chooses their own anonymity level, gets a new display name on every risk and sees their rank privately. When ACAPS records an outcome, forecasts earn points from a simple table, so each expert builds a track record in plain numbers.

We built on the ACAPS Risk Radar export of 139 risks and tested the scoring on the real outcomes of 104 past risks with 76 simulated experts. Rank closely matched the skill we set, which shows the scoring can find reliable experts. No real personal data is used.
