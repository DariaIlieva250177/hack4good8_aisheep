Rules at a glance

  • An expert gets a risk as a card when it matches an approved country or an approved domain. Country and domain counts 1.0, country only 0.6, domain only 0.4. Matching neither means the discussion can only be read.
  • Countries and domains are approved one by one; the moderator can deny any of them.
  • Account recovery works like a crypto wallet: a 12-word secret code, shown once. Only its hash is stored.
  • "Verified, then deleted" experts lose their name, organisation, email and documents after approval.
  • Display names are never stored with the expert: a new random name for every risk, kept within that risk's discussion.
  • An opinion can be changed once; the change shows as "Changed opinion" in the same discussion.
  • Triggers come from ACAPS. An expert who thinks one is missing says so in their answer.
  • An expert can send a risk statement as a suggested card. The moderator approves or rejects it, then posts it as a new risk.
  • Card colours: red (very urgent), orange (urgent), yellow (every other risk). There is no green.
